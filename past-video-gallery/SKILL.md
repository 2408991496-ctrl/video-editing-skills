# Past Video Gallery

Use this skill when the narration refers to earlier posts, a series of daily videos, or a creator's progress and the user wants the covers to move behind the speaker.

## Inputs
- Covers for the earlier videos, supplied by the user or already available in the project, in their true publishing order.
- The spoken phrase that motivates the gallery, its timing, the frame dimensions, and the speaker's movement range.
- A visual reference, if provided; otherwise follow the project's current visual style.

## Workflow
1. Verify the number and order of covers, their aspect ratios, and any readable labels. Do not assign a cover to the wrong day.
2. Present the covers as large vertical posters rather than small thumbnails. Give them subtle perspective, shadows, and horizontal movement. Advance them in order, leaving enough time to recognize each cover.
3. Let the posters approach the speaker's head height to create depth. Use an accurate foreground subject cutout or suitable mask to keep the head and face in front. If a clean cutout is unavailable, adjust the path and size to avoid the head; avoid a conspicuous circular halo around the person.
4. Align the carousel with the relevant narration. Optional page or passing sounds should stay quieter than the voice.
5. Animate the first entrance and final exit naturally, then clear the gallery before the next idea.

## Quality checks
Inspect the first cover, every changeover, the last cover, and frames with the most speaker movement. Confirm that the covers are large and legible, in the correct order, within the frame, and never cover the hair, forehead, eyes, or face.
